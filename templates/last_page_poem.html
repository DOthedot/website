THE LAST PAGE

YOU KNOW THAT MOMENT, HALF SAD, HALF SWEET
WHEN THE LAST PAGE MEETS THE EYE
THE WORLD YOU LIVED IN STEPS AWAY
AND YOU SIT THERE, SIGHING A SIGH

THE BACK COVER CLOSES LIKE A DOOR
BUT THE DOOR INSIDE STAYS OPEN STILL
SOME BOOKS DON'T END WHEN THE INK RUNS OUT
THEY WALK WITH YOU UP THE HILL

SO HERE'S TO THE LAST PAGE AND THE FIRST
TO EVERY LINE THAT MADE US FEEL
THE BOOK IS DONE, THE STORY STAYS—
TURN THE LAMP OFF, KEEP IT REAL.
