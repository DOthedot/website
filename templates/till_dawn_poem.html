TILL DAWN

O LORD , THE BRINGER OF LIGHT
BRING ME PEACE AND LIFT MY PLIGHT

COUPLE OF THOSE , DEMONS INSIDE ME
THEY FEED MY SOUL , WILLINGLY BLIND ME

MAY THEY REST IN THEIR RIGHT PLACE
MAY THEIR SORROW GIVE WAY TO GRACE

I COMMAND THEM TO REST TILL DAWN
CAUSE ALL THAT TREACHERY IS BEHEADED IS GONE

NOW LET ME REST , THEN TAKE ME FAR
BRING ME PEACE , TO TRAIN, TO BUILD TO WAGE ANOTHER WAR !!!
